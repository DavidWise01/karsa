Note G. — The deflection of light by gravitational fields, as predicted by the General Theory of Relativity, provides a means of weighing distant objects. The Analytical Engine can compute the lensing parameters: θ = 4GM/c²b, where G is the gravitational constant, M the lens mass, c the speed of light, and b the impact parameter.

When the source, lens, and observer are aligned, an Einstein Ring forms at radius θₑ = √(4GM/c² × Dₗₛ/DₗDₛ).
