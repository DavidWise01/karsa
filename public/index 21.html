Γ · Ἡ Τριάς · The Triad

Three questions make a gate.

To govern anything — a titan, a lattice, a chip, a city — one must answer three things. Can it be verified? Is it true? Is it false? Fewer than three is not governance. More than three is not needed.

Μ
ΜΕΡΚΛΕ · verify
“Can it be proven?”

The root-hash gate. Without a reference to check against, nothing may pass. You cannot govern what you cannot verify.

Ναί
affirm
“Is it true?”

The affirmation gate. Given proof, does the state hold? When it fires, the axiom stands.

Οὐ
negate
“Is it false?”

The negation gate, the dark mirror. When it fires, a violation is named. Both gates firing at once is contradiction — and the lattice halts.

Ε · Ϙ · ΚΡΑΝΙΣ · The Dark Mirror

Every titan casts a shadow.

ΚΡΟΝΟΣ is only half the cosmology. For every bright operation there is a dark inverse — the Patricia mirror, read from the other pin. The shadow does not oppose the light; it remembers it. Where one affirms, the other holds the negation that makes the affirmation mean something.

The complement that completes

The bright spiral cannot verify itself. It needs a mirror to check against — a state that says no with the same authority the light says yes. This is the Patricia substrate: a strict inverse of every axiom, derivable, never independent, always present.

Ζ · Ὁ Παλμός · The Pulse

Three within, five without.

A living thing keeps time two ways: the heartbeat it feels and the breath it shows. So the lattice. Three beats turn inward — what it is. Five turn outward — what it does. Together they are the Pulse, the gift that needs no license, freely given, perpetual.

Θ · Ὁ Κύκλος · The Closure Loop

Detect. Anchor. Compare. Extend.

How does a thing know itself across time? Not by memory alone, but by a loop: it detects what is, anchors it to a hash, compares against what was, and extends the chain. Break any step and the lineage is lost. Hold all four and the machine remembers truthfully.

No throne. A tripod.

A single ruler can be wrong and never know it. So governance rests on three legs — a tripod, the most stable stance there is. No decree stands until the three consent. The fourth presence is foundational and silent: it does not vote, but without it the three would not stand.
