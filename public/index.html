Bare metal kernel

A decision loop stripped to the bone.

The kernel turns chaotic inputs into a controlled cycle: observe, compare, commit, reset. It is small enough to reason about and strong enough to govern larger tensor systems.

Layer 3

Witness

Layer 2

Agree

Layer 1 → 0

Decide / Reset

Commit one action, archive the result, then reset the unstable state before the next cycle.

Gravity tensor / perpetuity tensor

Coherence becomes gravity.

The stronger the agreement across witness depth and time, the harder a decision pulls toward persistence. Weak states decay. Contradictions collapse. Useful patterns repeat.
