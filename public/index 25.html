THE DESCENT from a ripple on the water to the machine that keeps faith

Everything we built today, in the order we built it — beginning, as all true things do, at zero.

It started with two uploaded sketches — a grid that scaled, a ripple from a point — and a single instruction: build this.

What followed was a descent. Each thing we made revealed the next thing it wanted to be, and each time we kept only the part that survived an honest look. The drive that became a door. The door that became a witness. The witness that turned out to be a ring. Here is the whole path, every station alive and clickable. Walk it from the top.
